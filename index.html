Aravind Sunil Kumar

Senior Research Engineer at NVIDIA · Machine Learning for Physical AI and Robotic Systems

Building intelligence for robots in complex environments.

NVIDIA · Johns Hopkins University · IIT Madras

I’m a Senior Research Engineer at NVIDIA working across machine learning, Physical AI, robotic systems, and 3D perception. I’m particularly interested in how robots can perceive, represent, and interact with complex physical environments, including deformable scenes.

My experience in medical and surgical robotics includes the da Vinci Research Kit (dVRK), SlicerROS2, ROS 2, 3D Slicer, and image-guided robotic systems. At NVIDIA, I also contribute to Open-H efforts advancing open data, models, and tools for healthcare robotics.

I earned an MS in Computer Science from Johns Hopkins University, where I worked in the medical robotics community, and a B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering from IIT Madras. Earlier, I worked on VectorCam, a computer-vision system for malaria-vector surveillance that was featured by Bill Gates.

Work

Open-H and Physical AI

Contributing to efforts around open data, models, and research tools for AI-enabled surgical systems, with an emphasis on connecting machine learning research to real robotic workflows.

SlicerROS2 and dVRK

Open-source infrastructure connecting ROS 2 and 3D Slicer for image-guided robotics, alongside work in the dVRK ecosystem and surgical-robot simulation workflows.

VectorCam and VectorBrain

Computer-vision systems for scalable mosquito identification and malaria-vector surveillance, spanning deep-learning models, field imaging, and usability research in East Africa.
