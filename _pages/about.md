I'm a Senior Research Engineer at [NVIDIA](https://www.nvidia.com/) working across machine learning, Physical AI, robotic systems, and 3D perception. I'm particularly interested in how robots can perceive, represent, and interact with complex physical environments, including deformable scenes.

My experience in medical and surgical robotics includes the [da Vinci Research Kit (dVRK)](https://github.com/jhu-dvrk/sawIntuitiveResearchKit/wiki), [SlicerROS2](https://github.com/rosmed/slicer_ros2_module), ROS 2, 3D Slicer, and image-guided robotic systems. At NVIDIA, I also contribute to Open-H efforts advancing open data, models, and tools for healthcare robotics.

I earned an MS in Computer Science from [Johns Hopkins University](https://www.jhu.edu/), where I worked in the medical robotics community, and a B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering from [IIT Madras](https://www.iitm.ac.in/). Earlier, I worked on [VectorCam](https://vectorcam.org/), a computer-vision system for malaria-vector surveillance that was featured by [Bill Gates](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1GTTaWPl2ZE).
